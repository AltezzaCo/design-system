# Altezza visual language

Use a calm, premium operational canvas: obsidian and deep green surfaces, thin
dividers, and clear information hierarchy. Teal identifies actions and active
controls. Opportunity lime is scarce and meaningful: reserve it for success,
readiness, conversion, or a single primary marketing CTA.

Inter is the product and marketing typeface. Product UI favors compact,
information-dense composition; marketing headlines may scale dramatically while
body copy remains direct and easy to scan.

Ambient gradients, glow, and a fine grid can establish depth in hero areas, but
they must not compete with message content or appear on every card. Marketing and
authentication surfaces stay dark rather than switching to a white promotional
canvas.

The product story should show the operating loop, not a generic dashboard:
capture conversations, prioritize what needs attention, draft with context,
approve as a human, and follow through. Public channel claims must match the
channels that are actually available.
